How it works

Trust, transparency, results

The same checks for every seller, whether they are a five-year incumbent or a listing added last week. We are not a lab — we are the filter that reads the lab reports so you don't have to.

01

We verify the paperwork

A listing only qualifies if the seller publishes a certificate of analysis from a named third-party lab. We check the document is real, says which lab ran it, and ties to a specific lot rather than being one generic PDF reused across every batch.

02

We score what is checkable

Purity from the published results, COA transparency, consistency across lots, price and shipping, weighted into one score. Anything we cannot verify earns nothing — an unevidenced claim is not a point in a vendor’s favour.

03

You compare in one place

Every listing shows its score, links to the lab report behind it, and shows where the number came from. If a report stops checking out, the listing goes — it is not grandfathered in.

Get new test results first

Updates when we verify new lab reports or add a source. No vendor promotions.
